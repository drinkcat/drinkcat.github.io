Yesterday morning Singapore woke up under [thick haze](http://sg.news.yahoo.com/singapore-hit-haze-indonesia-forest-fires-060558314.html) due to forest fires in nearby Sumatra. [Not healthy](http://sg.news.yahoo.com/haze-reaches--unhealthy--level-in-singapore-085448213.html): You can feel it in your throat, and some corridors smell like [Scamorza](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scamorza) (some delicious Italian smoked cheese -- *Image: [Necrophorus](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Necrophorus) via [Wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Scamorza.jpg), GFDL license.*).
